How to read it: the ratio is the package's median cost per call divided by the fastest other row, so below 1 means the package is faster. A verdict compares conditional 95% intervals of the two medians: "leads" and "trails" mean the intervals do not overlap; "unresolved" means they do or the observation units differ. Platform rows are the runtime's own primitive; the package validates its output and returns typed errors, so that column is a floor, not a rival. Figures are one machine's and move between engine versions: read the direction of each difference, not its digits. Fresh-process rows use one median per process; their raw batches do not increase the independent sample count. Within-process rows assume independent batches and provide screening evidence only. Process samples still share scheduling and thermal conditions; neither mode establishes call p99.
